About

I am a PhD student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Zeyi Wen. I joined the Data Science and Analytics Thrust in Fall 2024. Before that, I studied at Sun Yat-sen University.

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning systems and high-performance computing. I am particularly interested in making full-parameter LLM fine-tuning efficient when model states exceed GPU memory: how to place data, schedule work, and use CPUs and GPUs together.

  • Efficient LLM training
  • Heterogeneous computing
  • Runtime scheduling
